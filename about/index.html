I got into photography because I wanted to capture beauty of the places that I was visiting. While the places themselves were special, the people I was with were more important. I hope that my photos capture the story of the people that have found themselves at those places. I have since taken my camera wtih me to Europe and many beautiful western states. When I am not hiking or backpacking to a spot for a perfect photo, you can find me listening to an audiobook while I am running, biking, or cooking.
